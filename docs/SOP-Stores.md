# Standard Operating Procedure — STORE / STOREKEEPER
### Karibu Pantry Planner

**App link:** https://palegoldenrod-coyote-386848.hostingersite.com
**Who this is for:** Camp storekeepers who receive requisitions from chefs and issue items.
**Tip:** Add the link to your phone home screen — it works like an app.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the app link.
2. Tap your **camp** (e.g. Ngorongoro Lions Paw).
3. Tap **your name** from the list.
4. Enter your **4-digit PIN** on the keypad.

> Forgot your PIN? Ask your admin to reset it. Do not share your PIN.

---

## 2. The order lifecycle (your part)

A chef's meal becomes an order that reaches you when it is **Submitted**. Your job is to issue the items and mark it **Fulfilled**.

| Stage | Meaning | Who acts |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Submitted | Chef sent the requisition to you | **Store opens it** |
| Fulfilled | You have issued the items | **Store** → chef notified |
| Received | Chef confirmed collection | Chef |
| Closed | Day finished | Chef |

You are notified (push + email) when a new requisition is submitted.

---

## 3. Fulfilling a requisition (the main job)

### Step 1 — Open the Store Dashboard
- The **Store Dashboard** shows three counters: **New Orders**, **Processing**, **Fulfilled Today**.
- Below that is **Incoming Requisitions** — the submitted orders waiting for you.

### Step 2 — Open the order
- Tap a requisition to open it. You'll see:
  - The **camp**, **meal**, **date**, and **guest count**.
  - The list of items with the **Requested** quantity.
  - Items are grouped; **Staple** items are tagged.

### Step 3 — Enter what you issue
- For each line, set the **Issuing** quantity (how much you are actually giving out).
- If you cannot supply an item, issue what you can — the difference is visible to the chef.
- If a line should not be there, you can **Remove** it (and **Restore** it if you change your mind).

### Step 4 — Mark as Fulfilled
- When everything is issued, tap **Mark as Fulfilled**.
- Status becomes **Fulfilled** and the chef is notified to come collect.

### Step 5 — Hand over to the chef
- The chef collects and confirms receipt in their app.
- Any difference between issued and received is recorded automatically.

---

## 4. The other pages

### Store Orders
- Full list of **Requisitions from kitchen** — current and past.
- Open any order to view detail, issue items, or remove/restore lines.
- Shows statuses: pending, fulfilled, received, rejected.

### Inventory (Store Stock)
- Adjust the store's physical stock (+/- with a reason).
- Keep this accurate so reports and availability are correct.

### History
- Past fulfilled requisitions for reference and checking.

### Reports
- Summaries of what's been ordered and issued over time.

---

## 5. Understanding the quantities

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Requested** | What the chef's menu needs (already minus their kitchen stock) |
| **Issuing** | What you actually give out — you set this |
| **Received** | What the chef confirms they collected |
| **Unused** | What the chef didn't use (recorded at day close) |

If **Issuing** is less than **Requested**, the chef sees the shortfall — communicate if an item is out of stock.

---

## 6. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| A new order isn't showing | Pull to refresh / reopen the app; check you're in the right camp. |
| I marked Fulfilled by mistake | Ask an admin — they can revert the order. |
| Wrong item on the order | Use **Remove** on that line before fulfilling; **Restore** if needed. |
| Didn't get the notification | Check **Store Dashboard → Incoming Requisitions** directly. |
| My name/PIN doesn't work | Ask your admin to check or reset your account. |

---

## 7. Do & Don't

**Do**
- Check the Store Dashboard at the start of each shift and after notifications.
- Enter the real **Issuing** quantity — not just copy the requested amount.
- Tell the chef when something is short or out of stock.
- Keep store inventory updated.

**Don't**
- Don't mark Fulfilled until items are physically ready to issue.
- Don't share your PIN.
- Don't delete a whole order — remove only the specific lines that don't apply.
