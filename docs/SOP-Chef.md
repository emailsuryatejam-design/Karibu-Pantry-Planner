# Standard Operating Procedure — CHEF
### Karibu Pantry Planner

**App link:** https://palegoldenrod-coyote-386848.hostingersite.com
**Who this is for:** Camp chefs who plan meals and send requisitions to the store.
**Tip:** Add the link to your phone home screen — it works like an app, even offline for viewing.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the app link.
2. Tap your **camp** (e.g. Ngorongoro Lions Paw).
3. Tap **your name** from the list.
4. Enter your **4-digit PIN** on the keypad.

> Forgot your PIN or name not listed? Ask your admin — they can reset it. Do not share your PIN.

---

## 2. The daily picture (how an order moves)

Every meal you plan becomes an **order** that travels through these stages:

| Stage | What it means | Who acts |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| **Draft** | Empty meal slot for the day | Chef builds it |
| **Locked / Processing** | You finished the menu, the item list is generated | Chef |
| **Submitted** | Sent to the store | Store receives it |
| **Fulfilled** | Store has issued the items | Chef collects |
| **Received** | You confirmed you collected the items | Chef |
| **Closed** | Day finished, unused items recorded | Chef |

You do this **per meal**: breakfast, lunch, dinner, lunchboxes, picnics, sundowner, bush dinner.

---

## 3. Building and sending a meal order (the main job)

### Step 1 — Open the Dashboard
- The **Dashboard** (home) shows today's meals.
- Pick the meal you are planning (tap the meal tab, e.g. *Dinner*).

### Step 2 — Set the number of guests
- Set the **guest count / pax** for that meal. Quantities scale to this number.

### Step 3 — Add your dishes
- Tap **add a dish** and search your recipes.
- Add every dish you will cook for that meal.
- As you add dishes, the app shows the **combined item list** it will order. Quantities already subtract what you have in the kitchen.

### Step 4 — Lock the meal
- When the menu is final, tap **Lock & Submit** (or **Lock**) for that meal.
- This freezes the menu and generates the order (status becomes **Processing**).

### Step 5 — Review on the Orders page
- Go to **Orders**.
- Two groups appear: **Menu Items** (from your recipes) and **Staple Items** (things like salt/oil you add manually if needed).
- Adjust any quantity if you need more or less.
- Add extra **staple items** here with **Add Item** if something isn't covered by a recipe.

### Step 6 — Submit to the store
- Tap **Submit** to send the order to the store.
- The store and camp manager are notified automatically.
- Status becomes **Submitted**. ⚠️ After submitting you cannot edit it — ask an admin to revert if you must change it.

### Step 7 — Collect and confirm receipt
- When the store marks the order **Fulfilled**, you get a notification.
- Go to **Review Supply** (or **Day Close**) → open the order → check what you actually received → tap **Confirm Receipt**.

### Step 8 — Close the day
- Go to **Day Close**.
- Enter any **Unused** quantities (what you didn't use), then **Save Unused** and **Close** the day.
- This keeps kitchen stock accurate for tomorrow.

## 4. Recipes — the most important rule

Your orders are only as good as your recipes. Open the **Recipes** page to manage them.

### Adding an ingredient
- Open a recipe → **add ingredient** → search the item → set the quantity.
- Checkbox **"Order from store"**:
  - **Checked** = the store will supply it (default).
  - **Unchecked** = it's a pantry staple you already keep (salt, oil) — it will NOT be ordered.

### The orange circle (toggle) — READ THIS
Each ingredient in a recipe has a round button on the left:

| Button | Meaning | Effect on orders |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| 🟠 **Orange (ticked)** | "Order from store" | **Will appear** in your requisition |
| ⚪ **Grey (crossed-out text)** | "Pantry staple" | **Will NOT be ordered** |

**Tap the circle to switch.** If you turn it **grey/off**, that item will not be ordered when you lock the menu — use this for things you already have in the kitchen.

> If you turned something off but still see it, **refresh the page** (close and reopen the app). The toggle is now respected everywhere.

### Removing an ingredient
- Tap the small **✕** next to the ingredient to remove it from the recipe completely.
  (Difference: the grey circle keeps it in the recipe but skips ordering; the ✕ removes it from the recipe.)

## 5. Kitchen Stock

- **Kitchen Stock** page lets you adjust what's physically in your kitchen (+/- with a reason).
- Keeping this updated means orders only ask for what you actually need (it subtracts current stock).

---

## 6. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| An item I switched off still shows | Close and reopen the app (hard refresh). |
| I can't edit a submitted order | Ask an admin to revert it to draft. |
| Wrong guest count after building | On the Orders page, change guests — quantities recalculate. |
| My name/PIN doesn't work | Ask your admin to check or reset your account. |
| I don't see the store's response | Wait for the notification, then check **Review Supply**. |

---

## 7. Do & Don't

**Do**
- Set the correct guest count *before* adding dishes.
- Keep recipes accurate — toggle off true pantry staples.
- Confirm receipt and record unused items at day close.

**Don't**
- Don't submit before reviewing the Orders page.
- Don't share your PIN.
- Don't assume a toggle change applies to an order you already locked — re-lock to regenerate.
